About the salon

CATRIN bridal fashion salon in Jelgava

Since 2011, we have helped brides find, create and alter dresses for their wedding day. At the salon, you can choose accessories, arrange dress care and, by prior agreement, leave the dress in storage until the wedding.

After the choice

To make the fitting more useful

What to bring

You may bring inspiration images, shoes with a similar heel height and the underwear you plan to wear. If you have not chosen these yet, no special preparation is needed.
